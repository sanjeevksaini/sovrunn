# Platform Sovereignty Composition

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Platform sovereignty could be asserted by geography alone or by a single policy check.

## Decision

Platform sovereignty evaluates one SovrunnInstallation and every dependency able to control, observe, change, decrypt, or recover it.

## Consequences

Sovereignty is evidence-backed, not geographically assumed. Every dependency must be assessed. SovereigntyProfile and SovereigntyFactSet compose the evaluation.

## Migration

No existing sovereignty assessment exists; this establishes the canonical pattern.

## Conformance Evidence

Geography alone never proves sovereignty in any active authority. Fresh/stale/missing/tampered evidence fixtures test the evaluation.
